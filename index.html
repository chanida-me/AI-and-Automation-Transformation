Phase 1: Foundation

Building the Foundation

NOW – Q2'27 | ~6–9 Months

🏛️

Governance & Sponsorship

  • Committee sponsored by CEO, CFO, and Regulator
  • Approves new investment & budget
  • Hub operates as an open space / safe zone for experimentation
⚙️

Hub Operating Model

AI & Automation Hub = Biz. Transformation + IT + Champion function

  • Consults on process & tool selection
  • Monitors progress & results
  • Shares AI success cases and demos
📢

Reporting & Cadence

  • Reports to management and updates employees regularly
  • Onsite/online sessions: Mon–Wed, 1:30–4:30pm
  • Standing MS Chat channel for continuous support
📚

Knowledge Training

BASIC

What tools are available in the market

ADVANCED

How to use each tool in real work

Governance Framework

  • Security: Do's & don'ts

  • Licensing: Individual vs. enterprise
  • Tools: Approved vs. prohibited list

  • Dashboard for tracking & visibility

  • PRIDE team to build awareness and keep momentum

Phase 2: Expansion

Expand Scope, Elevate Impact

Q3'27 – Q4'27 | 6 Months

Builds directly on the Phase 1 foundation — same committee and governance model, now going deeper.

🏆

AI Champion Evolution

Where possible, the AI Champion acts as the AI Hub within their own department — bringing the Hub model closer to daily work.

🎯 Goal: Create internal AI evangelists who drive adoption, answer questions, and identify new opportunities within their teams.

🔄

Governance Review

  • Revisit and refresh security, data, connection, and licensing policies
  • Based on Phase 1 learnings and real use-case experience
  • Expand approved tools list as market evolves
📖

Knowledge Refresh

🆕 NEW TOOLS

Train new tools for all employees as they reach the market

🔄 RE-TRAIN

Re-train and upskill AI Champions to keep pace

🚀

Use Case Expansion

Move beyond basic automation to more complex use cases, with a clear shift toward generating revenue or reducing cost.

💰 Business Impact Focus:

Generate Revenue Reduce Cost Improve Efficiency

Examples: Predictive analytics, intelligent document processing, customer service automation, supply chain optimization, sales forecasting.
